Newsynk · Account & privacy

Request account deletion

Newsynk users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time, even after uninstalling the app. You do not need to reinstall it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email letnewsynk@gmail.com with the subject Newsynk account deletion request.
  2. Include the email address used to sign in to Newsynk. Please do not send your password.
  3. We will verify that you own the account before deleting it and confirm when the request is complete.
Start deletion request

What is deleted

After verification, we delete your Newsynk sign-in account and account-linked profile, saved articles, preferences, comments, and votes from the services we control. Local app data can be removed by uninstalling Newsynk or clearing its storage.

What may be retained

Information required by law, security, or fraud-prevention obligations may be retained only for as long as necessary. We will explain any such retention when responding to your request. Third-party publishers' articles are not your account data and are not controlled by Newsynk.

You can also find this option in Newsynk under Profile → Request account deletion. For other privacy questions, email letnewsynk@gmail.com.

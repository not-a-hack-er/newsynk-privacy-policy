# Delete your Newsynk account

Newsynk users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time, even after uninstalling the app. You do not need to reinstall it.

## Request deletion

1. Email [letnewsynk@gmail.com](mailto:letnewsynk@gmail.com?subject=Newsynk%20account%20deletion%20request) with the subject **Newsynk account deletion request**.
2. Include the email address used to sign in to Newsynk. **Do not send your password.**
3. We will verify that you own the account before deleting it and confirm when your request is complete.

[Start a Newsynk account deletion request](mailto:letnewsynk@gmail.com?subject=Newsynk%20account%20deletion%20request&body=My%20Newsynk%20sign-in%20email%20is%3A%20)

## Data deleted

After verification, we delete the Newsynk sign-in account and account-linked profile, saved articles, preferences, comments, and votes from services we control. Local app data can be removed by uninstalling Newsynk or clearing its storage.

## Data that may be retained

Information required by law, security, or fraud-prevention obligations may be retained only for as long as necessary. We will explain any such retention when responding to your request. Third-party publishers' articles are not your account data and are not controlled by Newsynk.

You can also find this option in the Newsynk app under **Profile → Request account deletion**. For other privacy questions, email [letnewsynk@gmail.com](mailto:letnewsynk@gmail.com).

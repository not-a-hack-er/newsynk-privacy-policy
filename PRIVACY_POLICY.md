# Newsynk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Developer: Akarsh Bajpai · Android package: `com.abpvt.newsapp`_

Newsynk is a news aggregator. This policy describes the data handled by the app and the services it uses. We do not sell personal data or display advertising in Newsynk.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** Your email address, display name and authentication identifier when you register or sign in through email/password or Google Sign-In. Firebase Authentication handles passwords; Newsynk does not store them.
- **Content and preferences:** Bookmarks, article votes and comments are stored in Firebase Firestore so they can be available with your account. Your avatar colour, selected profile image, notification settings and some reading preferences may be stored on your device.
- **Device and notification identifiers:** Firebase may create installation identifiers. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device token to deliver notifications.
- **App health:** Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs, app state, device metadata and a Crashlytics installation identifier to diagnose failures. Firebase services may collect related diagnostics and installation data.
- **Optional analytics:** You can choose whether Firebase Analytics collection is enabled in the app's privacy settings. When enabled, it may collect app activity and device/installation data to help improve Newsynk.

We do not request precise location, contacts, phone numbers, payment details or broad access to your photo library. When you choose a profile photo, Android's content picker provides access to the selected image.

## How we use data

We use account information to sign you in, saved content and preferences to provide app features, notification tokens to deliver opted-in alerts, and crash/diagnostic data to fix bugs and improve reliability. Optional analytics is used only when enabled by you.

## Services and news sources

Firebase provides authentication, Firestore storage, messaging, app integrity checks, Crashlytics and optional analytics. Newsynk obtains news from third-party publishers and public feeds; articles open at their original sources. Those publishers apply their own privacy policies when you visit them. Depending on the app configuration, news sources may include The Guardian, BBC, GNews, Currents and NewsData.io.

## Security and retention

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. We keep account-linked data while your account remains active. Firebase diagnostic and backup retention can differ from account data retention. We do not make a promise that all service backups disappear immediately.

## Your choices and deletion

You can disable optional analytics and notification categories in Newsynk. To request deletion of your account and associated data, use the [Newsynk account deletion instructions](DELETE_ACCOUNT.md), including after uninstalling the app. We verify account ownership before deletion and normally complete requests within 30 days. If a legal or security obligation requires limited retention, we will explain what remains and for how long.
